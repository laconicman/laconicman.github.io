## Contact

The fastest way to reach the developer is a GitHub issue:
[github.com/laconicman/YDelivery/issues](https://github.com/laconicman/YDelivery/issues) —
bug reports, questions, and feature requests all land there. This is a
one-developer project; expect a reply within a few days.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
Yes — YDelivery works through your **Yandex Delivery business account** and its
API token (created in your Yandex business cabinet). The token lives in the
iOS Keychain; removing it signs the app out.

**Is this an official Yandex app?**
No. YDelivery is an unofficial client built on Yandex Delivery's public
Express API. Yandex doesn't endorse or support it — delivery questions about a
placed order belong to your provider's support; app questions belong here.

**Where does my data go?**
Your device and your private iCloud — see the
[privacy policy]({{ "/en/apps/ydelivery/privacy/" | relative_url }}).

**The courier didn't arrive / the price is wrong.**
That's a provider-side issue — contact Yandex Delivery support from your
business cabinet. If the *app* misbehaved (crash, wrong status, sync), file an
issue with a screenshot and the order's rough time.
