## Contact

The fastest way to reach the developer is a GitHub issue:
[github.com/laconicman/DiceLab/issues](https://github.com/laconicman/DiceLab/issues) —
bug reports, questions, and feature requests all land there. This is a
one-developer project; expect a reply within a few days.

## FAQ

**Do I need an account?**
No. DiceLab has no accounts and no sign-in — everything works offline.

**What are the two engines?**
The same dice table is rendered by SceneKit or RealityKit — switchable in
Settings. They simulate slightly different physics; that's the point of the
project.

**Where does my data go?**
Nowhere — the app has no network code. See the
[privacy policy]({{ "/en/apps/dicelab/privacy/" | relative_url }}).
