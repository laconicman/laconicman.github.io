## What the app collects

**Nothing.** DiceLab collects no data and sends nothing off your device.
The app makes no network requests at all — there is no account, no analytics,
no advertising, no tracking.

## What stays on the device

- **Settings** — dice count, engine choice, dynamics, appearance — stored in
  the app's preferences.
- **Backdrop photo** — only if you pick one for a custom theme; it stays in
  the app's container.
- **Roll history** — recent totals, kept on-device.

Everything above lives inside the app's sandbox and is deleted with the app.
